# OrbitDesk Product Overview

## What is OrbitDesk?

OrbitDesk is a customer support and helpdesk SaaS platform that helps teams
manage, triage, and resolve customer support tickets from a single workspace.
It combines a shared inbox, ticket automation, a knowledge base, and analytics
in one product.

## Core Features

- **Shared Inbox**: All support channels (email, chat, social) arrive in one
  place.
- **Ticket Management**: Create, assign, prioritise, and close tickets.
- **Automation**: Rules and workflows to auto-route and auto-respond to
  tickets.
- **Knowledge Base**: Build self-serve help articles for your customers.
- **Analytics**: Dashboards for response time, resolution time, and CSAT.
- **Integrations**: Connect to Slack, Salesforce, Zendesk, and more.

## Workspaces

OrbitDesk is organised into **workspaces**. Each workspace is an isolated
environment with its own team members, tickets, and settings. A user can belong
to multiple workspaces.

## Plans

OrbitDesk offers three subscription plans:

- **Starter**: For small teams. Includes up to 5 team members and 500 tickets
  per month.
- **Growth**: For scaling teams. Includes up to 20 team members and unlimited
  tickets.
- **Enterprise**: For large organisations. Includes unlimited members, SSO,
  advanced security, and priority support.
